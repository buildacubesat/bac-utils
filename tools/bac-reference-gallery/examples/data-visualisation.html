* Reserve green/red for genuine pass/fail meaning when the chart carries semantic state. Extend with tints instead of confusing semantics.
GOOD
Direct labels, units, restrained grid, dual encoding.
AVOID
Rainbow palettes, unlabelled axes, hue-only distinctions, decorative 3D.
MISSION · DARK CHART SURFACE
Thermal profile
Derived lighter shades may be used to maintain graphical contrast.
GRAPHICAL CONTRAST
Aim for at least 3:1 for meaningful lines, marks and UI glyphs.
IDENTITY PRESERVED
A lighter shade may represent the same canonical series on a dark surface.
