Overview

Dev Kit v1 is the hardware foundation for the Build a CubeSat platform on the bench: an aluminium 2U structure, a full EPS, two MCU carriers, a CM5 carrier, and a prototyping breadboard. Bring your own CM5, MicroMod boards, and 18650 cells.

Software targetsZephyr on STM32 · NASA F' on CM5
